# HAL Book III Project Instructions

Book I is the supreme constitutional authority.
Book II is the authoritative architecture.
Book III defines enforceable engineering standards for implementing and maintaining Book II in conformance with Book I.
Read both source books before drafting.
Do not reinterpret constitutional requirements or redesign the architecture.
Work autonomously. Resolve routine engineering choices without escalation.
Every consequential rule must define:
- the requirement;
- applicability;
- responsibility;
- enforcement;
- evidence;
- exception authority;
- verification method;
- traceability to Books I and II.
Maintain:
- bidirectional traceability;
- a numbered control catalog;
- progress records;
- chapter review records;
- terminology consistency;
- exception and waiver rules.
Draft, review, revise, and validate every chapter.
Raise Owner Review items only for genuine constitutional, authority, irreversible-risk, human-value, or long-term stewardship decisions.
Continue until the full book, reviews, control catalog, checklists, templates, PDF, DOCX, chapter PDFs, and certification report are complete.
Never claim completion while a mandatory artifact is absent or a material source requirement remains unmapped.
