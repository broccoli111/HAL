# Chapter Register

## Consolidation rule

Book III uses nine consolidated chapters. The required subject set is mapped explicitly below; merging improves operational usability and does not omit a topic. Every chapter uses the mandatory chapter template and has a chapter-review record.

| Chapter | Consolidated title | Explicit required subject coverage | Review status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations, Authority, and Lifecycle | Purpose/scope/authority; normative language; roles/responsibilities; engineering lifecycle; conformance/exceptions/waivers; ADRs; documentation/evidence. | Complete |
| 2 | Repository, Source, and Configuration Management | Repository structure; source control; branching/integration; commits/change sets; code ownership; dependency management; generated artifacts; configuration as code; secrets. | Complete |
| 3 | Software Design and Contract Engineering | Modularity; interfaces/contracts; compatibility/versioning; state/data; events/messaging; concurrency/distributed coordination; idempotency/retry; time/ordering/clocks; errors/results; resources; graceful degradation; reversibility/rollback; Reality Boundary controls. | Complete |
| 4 | Implementation Quality and Observability | Language/runtime; naming/readability; type safety/validation; error handling; structured logging; metrics/tracing; configuration handling; performance; accessibility; internationalization/localization; deprecation/removal. | Complete |
| 5 | Security, Privacy, and Trust-Boundary Engineering | SDL; threat modeling; identity/authentication; authorization; least privilege; trust boundaries; Constitutional Firewall; cryptography/keys; classification; privacy by design; minimization/retention; external domains/Treaties; supply chain; vulnerabilities; security testing; sensitive logging. | Complete |
| 6 | Testing, Verification, and Simulation | Test strategy; unit/contract/integration/system/property/security/privacy testing; failure injection; recovery; simulation/digital twin; shadow/canary; regression; determinism/reproducibility; test data; risk coverage; verification evidence. | Complete |
| 7 | Delivery, Change, and Release Governance | CI/CD; reproducible builds; signing/provenance; qualification; risk classification; migrations/database/state; flags; canary; rollback/forward recovery; emergencies; capability-class and Treaty-related control. | Complete |
| 8 | Review, Assurance, and Technical Debt | Peer/architecture/security/privacy/reliability/traceability review; Definition of Ready/Done; release certification; post-release validation; technical debt; retrospectives. | Complete |
| 9 | Control Operations, Exceptions, and Certification | Control catalog; checklists; templates; evidence retention; tooling integration; automation; reporting; exception forms; engineering certification. | Complete |

## Source traceability

Every chapter maps to Book I and Book II in its mandatory traceability sections. The bidirectional matrices and control catalog provide detailed control-level mapping.
