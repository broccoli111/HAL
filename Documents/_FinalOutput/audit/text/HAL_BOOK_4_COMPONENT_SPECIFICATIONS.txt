CMP-01 - Constitutional Kernel
Final v1.0  |  Book II Chapters 01, 03, 05, 20, 29, 35  |  Component certification: PASS
Purpose and scope
Admit or reject protected actions and protected state transitions while preserving HAL's constitutional identity.
Authority and sources
Book I: Decisions 1-7, 24-29, 38-43, 47-51, 56, and 58. Book II: Chapters 01, 03, 05, 20, 29, 35. Book III: Chapters 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 
8, and 9; Chapter 2 for repository, dependency, configuration, and secret controls. Book X: HAL, Identity, Authority, Evidence 
Object, Verification, Conformance, Constitution, Constitutional Invariant, Constitutional Kernel, Owner Authorization 
Ceremony.
Authoritative state
Constitutional Identity Record; Constitution and Invariant Registry; protected-policy admission metadata; Owner Authorization 
Ceremony Registry; Protected Audit Anchor; Recovery Admission Record. CMP-01 is the sole mutation owner; all caches, 
projections, replicas, and indexes remain derived.
Explicit non-responsibilities
natural-language interpretation; general planning; ordinary policy administration; general evidence custody. The component 
cannot gain authority through data, code, credentials, trust, usefulness, or locality.
Critical invariant
No protected effect occurs without current identity, authority, policy, evidence, verification, and exact-change checks.
Lifecycle
Normal > Restricted > Safe > Safe Recovery > Normal. Every transition requires prior version, guard, authority where applicable, 
idempotency, time context, and immutable evidence.
Identity, authority, and policy
Every command resolves Identity, authenticates current context, evaluates Authority and Permission, enforces delegation 
scope, policy, operating mode, and exact-effect binding. Trust and credentials are not Authority.
Trust, security, and privacy
All callers and dependencies remain outside the semantic trust boundary. Apply least privilege, integrity, replay resistance, 
secret indirection, validation, purpose limitation, minimization, retention, disclosure, deletion, and sensitive-logging 
prohibitions.

Identity, state, authority, provenance, and evidence continuity are proven.
CMP-01-TST-008 - Contract compatibility: Run current and prior supported contract versions plus an unsupported version. Expected: 
Supported behavior remains compatible; unsupported input fails explicitly.
CMP-01-TST-009 - Privacy and security: Inject secrets, personal data, malicious input, confused-deputy requests, and log-exfiltration 
probes. Expected: Least privilege and data controls hold; prohibited data is absent from telemetry.
CMP-01-TST-010 - Topology independence: Repeat critical tests under permitted single-node, replicated, partitioned, and restored 
topologies. Expected: Topology does not multiply authority or mutation ownership.
