# Terminology Control

Book X is the semantic authority. Book VI uses Book X terms without redefining them.

Operational terms introduced here are subordinate program terms:

- **Control owner:** role accountable for a control's design, operation, evidence, and remediation.
- **Control operator:** role that performs the control.
- **Control assessor:** independent role that evaluates control effectiveness.
- **Security incident:** event that threatens confidentiality, integrity, availability, provenance, identity, or control effectiveness.
- **Authority incident:** event in which HAL acts, attempts to act, or could act outside valid authority.
- **Privacy incident:** event involving unauthorized, excessive, misleading, or purpose-incompatible processing.
- **Trust incident:** event that invalidates or materially weakens assumptions about a Trust Domain, Treaty, counterparty, or exchange path.

These terms do not change constitutional or architectural definitions.
