# Book VI Production Plan

Status: In progress
Version target: 1.0

## Authority

1. Book I — Constitution
2. Book II — Architecture Specification
3. Book III — Engineering Standards
4. Book IV — Component Specifications

6. Book VI — Security, Privacy, and Trust Manual

## Work phases

1. Inventory and assess authoritative sources.
2. Extract security, privacy, trust, authority, evidence, and incident obligations.
3. Establish terminology, roles, risk levels, control schema, exceptions, and reconciliation registers.
4. Draft the complete controlled manual and companion controls.
5. Produce templates, checklists, traceability, reviews, and certification records.
6. Generate Markdown, DOCX, PDF, and control workbook.
7. Run structural, semantic, constitutional, architecture, and publication validation.
8. Inspect every rendered page and workbook sheet; correct defects.
9. Conduct a fresh final audit and certify only if no material defect remains.

## Completion gate

Completion requires complete subject coverage, numbered controls, bidirectional traceability, explicit Book VIII/IX reconciliation, full reviews, resolved internally actionable findings, validated final artifacts, and documented Owner Review status.
