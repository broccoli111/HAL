# Major Incident Playbook

1. Declare the incident and assign Incident Commander, evidence custodian, operations lead, security/privacy lead, and independent verifier.
2. Authenticate identities; confirm narrow incident Authority and separation of duties.
3. Contain new protected actions and affected exchanges; preserve human communication under declared harm, privacy, and integrity controls.
4. Capture immutable chronology, state, Authority, Treaty, contract, certification, and outcome evidence.
5. Determine actual state; never infer success from timeout, credential possession, or process liveness.
6. Protect people, privacy, constitutional invariants, and reversibility; disclose material limitations.
7. Reconcile state and Reality Boundary effects; revoke compromised or temporary access.
8. Require targeted Book VIII reverification and explicit certification disposition before reliance resumes.
9. Communicate resolution, corrective actions, monitoring, and evidence retention.
