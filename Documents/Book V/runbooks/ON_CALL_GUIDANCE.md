# On-Call Guidance

The on-call role provides timely operational response; it does not confer constitutional or change authority.

## Start of shift

1. Authenticate with the assigned personal identity; shared operator identities are prohibited.
2. Review current runtime and constitutional modes, open incidents, active changes, degraded services, Treaties, certification states, temporary access, evidence gaps, capacity risks, and expiring certificates.
3. Confirm paging, secure communications, evidence capture, escalation contacts, and access to required runbooks.
4. Reject an incomplete handoff and escalate until material uncertainty is resolved.

## During shift

- Acknowledge Critical alerts within five minutes and High alerts within fifteen minutes.
- Declare an incident when scope, harm, authority, privacy, trust, integrity, or recovery risk is material or uncertain.
- Use the controlling runbook. Do not improvise privileged commands merely because access exists.
- Preserve evidence and communicate material limitations accurately.
- Never retry an ambiguous Reality Boundary action before reconciliation.
- Suspend protected reliance when certification, authority, Treaty, identity continuity, or evidence validity cannot be proven.

## End of shift

Complete the handoff template, identify every unresolved risk and deadline, transfer evidence custody explicitly, verify the receiving operator, and revoke any shift-specific temporary access.
