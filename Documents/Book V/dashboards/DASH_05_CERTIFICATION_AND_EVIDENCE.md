# Certification And Evidence

**Owner:** Operations Assurance Lead

Required panels:
- certificate scope/state: current value, threshold, freshness, source, scope, and evidence link.
- expirations: current value, threshold, freshness, source, scope, and evidence link.
- suspensions: current value, threshold, freshness, source, scope, and evidence link.
- evidence validity: current value, threshold, freshness, source, scope, and evidence link.
- drift: current value, threshold, freshness, source, scope, and evidence link.
- recertification triggers: current value, threshold, freshness, source, scope, and evidence link.
- corrective actions: current value, threshold, freshness, source, scope, and evidence link.

Unknown or stale values MUST render as unknown, never healthy. Every panel MUST link to the controlling runbook and alert.
