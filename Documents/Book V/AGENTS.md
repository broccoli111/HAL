# HAL Book V Project Instructions

Book I is the supreme constitutional authority.
Book II is the authoritative architecture.
Book III defines mandatory engineering standards.
Book IV defines component obligations and ownership.
Book IX defines machine-facing contracts.
Book VI governs the continuing security, privacy, and trust program.
Book VIII governs verification and certification.
Book X defines canonical terminology.

Book V defines repeatable procedures and controls for safely operating HAL. It MUST NOT create constitutional authority, redesign components, redefine contracts, weaken engineering controls, invent certification authority, or alter canonical meaning.

Read Books I-IV and X before drafting. Reconcile the final manual against Books VI, VIII, and IX before publication.

Every operational procedure MUST define:

- purpose, scope, prerequisites, and applicability;
- responsible and approving roles;
- required identity, authority, and separation of duties;
- exact ordered actions and decision points;
- preconditions, safety checks, and stop conditions;
- expected state transitions;
- evidence and telemetry;
- rollback, forward recovery, and escalation;
- degraded and failure behavior;
- security, privacy, trust, and Reality Boundary considerations;
- verification and completion criteria;
- traceability to controlling books;
- exceptions and expiration.

No procedure may treat possession of credentials, physical access, process liveness, deployment success, or administrative privilege as constitutional authority.

Do not finalize Book V until the Book VI, VIII, and IX reconciliation registers are closed.
