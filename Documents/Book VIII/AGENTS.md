# HAL Book VIII Project Instructions

Book I is the supreme constitutional authority. Book II is the authoritative architecture. Book III supplies mandatory engineering controls. Book IV supplies component obligations. Book X supplies canonical semantics.

Book VIII defines how claims are verified, how evidence is assembled, and how components, capabilities, releases, and Treaties are certified, suspended, and revoked. It MUST NOT reinterpret Book I, redesign Book II, weaken Book III, alter Book IV ownership, or redefine Book X terminology.

Book IX is the authoritative interface and protocol reference. Book VIII MUST use its registered contracts without inventing alternative wire contracts. Every assurance artifact or status not represented by a dedicated Book IX contract MUST use a documented, fail-closed mapping or enter the Book IX amendment process in `planning/BOOK_IX_RECONCILIATION_REGISTER.md`.

Every consequential rule MUST identify applicability, accountable role, verification method, required evidence, decision authority, failure consequence, and exception constraints. Constitutional invariants and failed authority controls are not waivable.

Maintain bidirectional traceability, a numbered verification and certification catalog, durable progress records, chapter reviews, explicit Owner Review findings, and publication validation. Do not claim completion while a mandatory artifact is absent or a material source obligation is unmapped.
