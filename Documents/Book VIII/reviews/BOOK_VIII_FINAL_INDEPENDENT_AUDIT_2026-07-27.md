# Book VIII Final Independent Audit

Date: 2026-07-27  
Edition audited: v1.0 Final  
Decision: PASS — CERTIFIABLE AS THE FINAL BOOK VIII MANUAL

## Scope

The audit examined 13 chapters, 104 numbered controls, 8 controlled templates, 7 checklists, 4 registers, 6 bidirectional source matrices, 13 chapter reviews, the source-integrity manifest, 10 Book IX v1.0 reconciliation mappings, the canonical Markdown/DOCX/PDF editions, 13 standalone chapter PDFs, and the six-sheet catalog workbook.

## Constitutional audit

PASS. Book VIII preserves Book I as supreme, makes evidence prior to confidence, requires progressive verification before real effects, protects Owner authority, privacy, dignity, restraint, continuity, and recoverability, and forbids waiver of constitutional invariants. Certification is explicitly scoped, expiring, and unable to create Authority, Permission, Owner approval, a capability class, or Treaty scope.

No constitutional conflict was found.

## Architecture audit

PASS. The manual uses the Book II Verification Service, Evidence Service, Simulation and Digital Twin Platform, Constitutional Kernel, Constitutional Firewall, Capability Registry, Runtime Supervisor, Recovery Coordinator, and observability responsibilities without changing component boundaries, authoritative-state ownership, or deployment topology.

All 29 Book IV components are covered by a common component-certification model. Book VIII does not duplicate component specifications.

## Engineering and semantic audit

PASS. Each consequential control has a stable identifier, applicability, accountable role, enforcement, evidence, severity, exception authority, source mapping, chapter, and automation status. Controls remain risk-based and objectively reviewable. Book X distinctions among Identity, Authority, Permission, Trust, Evidence, Verification, Certification, Capability, Treaty, and Reality Boundary are preserved.

## Assurance completeness

PASS. Claims, evidence, assurance cases, risk classes, budgets, confidence, fidelity, reproducibility, counterfactuals, failure injection, recovery, human verification, continuous verification, regression, component, capability, release, deployment, and Treaty certification are governed. Certification conditions, expiry, suspension, revocation, status propagation, dependency invalidation, and reinstatement are explicit.

## Security, privacy, trust, and safety

PASS. The manual separately verifies controls that protect HAL and controls that prevent HAL from exceeding Authority. It assumes compromised components, tests authority and trust-boundary negatives, governs evidence minimization and custody, and requires Treaty and Constitutional Firewall enforcement.

## Book IX contract reconciliation

PASS. Book VIII was reopened after Book IX v1.0 became final. Every explicit Book IX contract ID cited by Book VIII exists in the final catalog.

Nine mappings are directly resolved:

- Evidence admission, retrieval, integrity, challenge, and supersession: IX-C-0174–0183.
- Verification requests, results, confidence, defeaters, reproduction, and invalidation: IX-C-0143–0153.
- Simulation, Digital Twin, fidelity, isolation, and failure injection: IX-C-0154–0164.
- Authority attestations: IX-C-0022 and IX-C-0024–0029.
- Reality Boundary action and protected-admission evidence: IX-C-0131–0142 and IX-C-0001–0008.
- Audit and observability evidence: IX-C-0241–0251.
- All-component conformance: all 305 registered contracts and IX-CMP-001–003.
- Treaty and Constitutional Firewall assurance: IX-C-0196–0217 and IX-TRT-001–003.
- Invalidation, recovery, and regression triggers: IX-C-0145, IX-C-0153, IX-C-0250–0251, and IX-C-0264–0275.

Book IX v1.0 has no dedicated certification-status query or lifecycle event. Book VIII resolves this safely by admitting a signed Certification Decision as an Evidence Object, superseding it for state changes, appending an audit record, and publishing a correlated event through existing Book IX contracts. Runtimes retrieve and integrity-check the current Evidence Object and fail closed when its status, freshness, or applicability cannot be proven. `IXA-001` records the ordinary Book IX extension for dedicated certification-status query and event contracts.

This composition does not invent a wire contract or alter component ownership. The extension is an interface-governance improvement, not an Owner Review item and not a blocker to Book VIII as a manual.

## Owner Review audit

No Owner Review item is open. Book VIII correctly reserves future Owner decisions for actual new capability classes, Treaty approvals or classes, constitutional interpretation, substantial irreversible risk, major human-value conflicts, and long-term stewardship choices. It does not make any such decision itself.
