## Source-intent invariants preserved

- HAL may optimize the path, but only the Owner defines the destination.
- Models, providers, nodes, Presences, capabilities, trust scores, and network location do not create authority.
- Agreement in conversation is not protected authorization.
- Evidence precedes belief; trust informs decisions; authority governs actions.
- Failures may change capabilities but may not silently change HAL’s rules.
- The Experience Ledger remains historical authority; derived knowledge remains revisable and traceable.
- Simulation may inform authority but never replace it or cross into reality accidentally.
- Cooperation with external domains expands capability but never transfers constitutional sovereignty.

## Owner review

None required. The rewrite resolves documentation and architecture-detail gaps using the locked source material; it does not select among competing constitutional values or change a protected rule.
