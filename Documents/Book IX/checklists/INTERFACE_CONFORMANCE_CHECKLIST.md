# Interface Conformance Checklist

- [ ] Registered contract and Book IV source match.
- [ ] Envelope and payload validate.
- [ ] Identity and current authority are verified.
- [ ] Idempotency, retries, timeouts, cancellation, and ambiguity are safe.
- [ ] Errors and partial results are explicit.
- [ ] Ordering, limits, compatibility, and deprecation are declared.
- [ ] Privacy, classification, redaction, telemetry, and evidence rules pass.
- [ ] Treaty and Firewall checks pass for every cross-domain path.
- [ ] Positive and adversarial contract tests pass.
