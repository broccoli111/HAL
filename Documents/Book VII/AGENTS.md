# HAL Book VII Project Instructions

Book I is the supreme constitutional authority and the source of Owner authority.
Book II defines architecture governance boundaries.
Book III governs engineering policy and exceptions.
Books V, VI, and VIII define operational, security/trust, and certification processes that Book VII governs institutionally.
Book X defines canonical terminology.

Book VII defines how human governance is exercised over time. It MUST NOT expand Owner authority, convert administrative roles into constitutional authority, create a new capability or Treaty class, redefine certification, or override operational and technical evidence.

Read Book I completely before drafting. Consult all other final books and reconcile governance roles, approvals, exceptions, appeals, emergency powers, succession, suspension, revocation, evidence, and retention before publication.

Every governance procedure MUST define:

- decision class and controlling authority;
- proposer, reviewer, approver, recorder, and affected parties;
- separation of duties and conflict-of-interest controls;
- inputs, evidence threshold, deliberation record, and decision output;
- effective date, review date, sunset, revocation, and appeal;
- emergency limits and retrospective review;
- publication, confidentiality, and retention;
- traceability to the Constitution and applicable subordinate manuals.

Owner Review is reserved for the thresholds established by Book I and may not be manufactured by lower-order process design.

Do not finalize Book VII until Books V, VI, and VIII are final and cross-book authority reconciliation is closed.
