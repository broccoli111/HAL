# HAL Canon Final Integration Review

**Status:** Not yet executed  
**Scope:** Books I-X

## Required evidence

- Locked source hash verification
- Final certification report from every book
- Open-finding and Owner Review registers
- Bidirectional cross-book traceability
- Cross-book term comparison against Book X
- Authority and approval matrix
- Lifecycle and state reconciliation
- Interface-to-operation-to-verification mappings
- Security/privacy/trust-to-operation mappings
- Certification-to-governance mappings
- Complete deliverable manifest
- DOCX, PDF, and workbook visual inspection records

## Audit questions

### Constitutional

- Does any lower book create, weaken, waive, reinterpret, or bypass a constitutional invariant?
- Does any role acquire Owner authority by title, credential, possession, deployment privilege, or process?
- Is every Reality Boundary action authorized, verified, attributable, reversible where possible, and evidenced?

### Architecture and ownership

- Does every authoritative state domain retain exactly one mutation owner?
- Do contracts, procedures, and governance records preserve Book II and Book IV boundaries?
- Are degraded, recovery, and distributed modes topology-independent and non-authorizing?

### Semantic

- Does every shared term retain its Book X meaning?
- Are qualified terms used where one word has multiple legitimate senses?
- Are deprecated and forbidden ambiguous terms absent from normative requirements?

### Interfaces

- Does every operational or verification dependency reference a final Book IX contract?
- Does Book IX define machine semantics without creating architecture?
- Are authority context, identity, causation, correlation, provenance, time/freshness, idempotency, and evidence represented where required?

### Verification and certification

- Does every material claim have criteria, method, environment, evidence, uncertainty, and reproducibility requirements?
- Are certification authority, scope, duration, suspension, and revocation consistent across Books V-VIII?
- Can every critical invariant and failure mode be tested?

### Security, privacy, and trust

- Are protections of HAL distinguished from controls preventing HAL from exceeding authority?
- Are Treaty, Constitutional Firewall, external-domain, privacy, and incident rules consistent?
- Are emergency and break-glass mechanisms time-bounded, attributable, least-privileged, and retrospectively reviewed?

### Operations and governance

- Do operational procedures preserve technical and constitutional stop conditions?
- Are governance decisions evidence-bearing, appealable where required, time-bounded, and recorded?
- Do emergency powers expire and receive independent review?

### Publication

- Are all required artifacts present and final?
- Did every DOCX and PDF page and every workbook sheet pass visual inspection?
- Are there no placeholders, broken references, missing glyphs, clipped tables, or stale status markers?

## Decision

The full canon MUST NOT be certified until all material findings are resolved or recorded as genuine Owner Review items.
